# Northbridge Cyber & Business Policy — 2026

## Cyber Liability

The Cyber Liability sublimit is CAD 2,000,000 per claim. The deductible is CAD 25,000.

## Business Interruption

The Business Interruption sublimit is CAD 750,000. The deductible is CAD 15,000 and the waiting period remains 24 hours before indemnity begins.

## Data Breach Response

Data Breach Response provides CAD 400,000 toward forensic investigation, notification, and credit-monitoring costs. The deductible is CAD 5,000.

## Policy Period

Effective date: 2026-01-01. Expiration date: 2027-01-01.
