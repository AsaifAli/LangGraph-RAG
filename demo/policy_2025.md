# Northbridge Cyber & Business Policy — 2025

## Cyber Liability

The Cyber Liability sublimit is CAD 1,000,000 per claim. The deductible is CAD 25,000.

## Business Interruption

The Business Interruption sublimit is CAD 500,000. The deductible is CAD 10,000 and the waiting period is 24 hours before indemnity begins.

## Data Breach Response

Data Breach Response provides CAD 250,000 toward forensic investigation, notification, and credit-monitoring costs. The deductible is CAD 5,000.

## Policy Period

Effective date: 2025-01-01. Expiration date: 2026-01-01.
